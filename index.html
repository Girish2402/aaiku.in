Our Story

Born from a Passion
for Perfection

Aaiku was founded with a singular vision — to create jewellery that transcends time. Each piece is a celebration of India's rich goldsmithing heritage, reimagined for the modern connoisseur.

We source only conflict-free, certified diamonds and the finest 18K and 22K gold, ensuring that every Aaiku creation is not just beautiful, but ethically impeccable.

From bridal masterpieces to everyday elegance, our artisans pour hundreds of hours of skill into each creation, producing jewellery that becomes part of your story.
